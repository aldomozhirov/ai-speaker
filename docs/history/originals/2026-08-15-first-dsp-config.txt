IMPORTANT SAFETY
1. Disconnect the tweeter while commissioning the DSP.
2. Put a 33 uF non-polar/bipolar capacitor rated at least 100 V in series with one tweeter lead.
3. Do not bridge Amp4 channels, join speaker negative terminals, or connect a speaker terminal to ground.
4. All players and TTS services must output to ALSA device `smartspk` (or the configured ALSA default), never directly to the HiFiBerry hardware device.
5. The starter crossover must be measured and tuned in the completed enclosure.

FILES
- smart-speaker.yml -> /etc/camilladsp/smart-speaker.yml
- asound.conf -> /etc/asound.conf
- camilladsp.service -> /etc/systemd/system/camilladsp.service

Before use, replace `sndrpihifiberry` in the YAML and ALSA files if `aplay -l` reports a different card ID.
